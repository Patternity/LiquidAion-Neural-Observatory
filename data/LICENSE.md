# FlyWire-derived data attribution and terms

`data/cx512` is an adapted subset of FlyWire FAFB public release 783, obtained
from public Codex exports. Directed pair counts were summed across neuropils,
and an induced strongly connected subset was selected. No artificial edges were added.
